Copy a node's URL from its actions menu or Find to paste into an agent or reopen later. Paste it back into Dotflowy to create a native link with a live title and backlink when the target exists in your outline. In an empty node without children, choose Link to node or Mirror node; dismissing keeps the link, and unsafe mirrors are disabled. Copying a link does not share the node or change who can access it.
