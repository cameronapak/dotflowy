Open a bullet's actions from the mouse, keyboard, or phone. Hover a row and click the ⋯ at its end, press `Cmd/Ctrl+Shift+.` while editing, or tap ⋯ in the phone's editing bar. The menu lists the same actions as Find's "Acting on" list: move, mirror, complete, zoom, delete, and plugin commands.
