Open a bullet's actions from the mouse or keyboard. Hover a row and click the ⋯ at its end, or press `Cmd/Ctrl+Shift+.` while editing. The menu lists the same actions as Find's "Acting on" list: move, mirror, complete, zoom, delete, and plugin commands. The `/` menu also gains Move up, Move down, Collapse, Expand, and Zoom in, so phones reach them from the editing bar's `/` button.
