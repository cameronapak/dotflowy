Pick a color theme in Settings → Appearance or from the command palette: Dotflowy, Claude-ish, Catppuccin, Twitter-ish, Modern Minimal, or Leaf. Your theme follows you across devices, and light and dark mode work with every theme. Tag, highlight, and link colors now blend with the theme's background, quote bars and empty checkboxes are easier to see, and the focus band now drains like the pop-out window.
